Garnet is my first try in trying to draw and rig a Live2D model. My aim was to find out if this technology was something we could use for our game graphics in the future, so I drew this character and rigged her for practice. 
 I don't think I have any further use for this particular model, so do what you will with her! Utilize her in your own projects, start vtubing or learn rigging from my mistakes :) 
 The model is compatible with the free version of Live2D Cubism. 
 You can download both the cubism model and the photoshop file, if you want to try your hand at rigging it yourself.
